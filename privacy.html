Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Cheddar & Sid Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @orangecarched. This policy explains how the app uses Google user data.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Drive: lists, downloads, and deletes video files in one designated Drive folder owned by the channel owner.
  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and checks their upload status.

How the data is used

Data is used only to move videos from the owner's Drive folder to the owner's YouTube channel. The app does not access any other files, accounts, or channels.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. Video files are downloaded temporarily for upload and then deleted from that computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising. The only third-party service involved is Anthropic's Claude API, which receives the video's title text in order to write a short description. No video files or account information are sent to it.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
