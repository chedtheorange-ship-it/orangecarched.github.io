Cheddar & Sid Uploader

Cheddar & Sid Uploader is a personal automation tool that posts short cat videos to the YouTube channel @orangecarched.

Once a day, it takes the oldest video from a single Google Drive folder owned by the channel owner, uploads it to the channel's own YouTube account with a title and short description, confirms the upload succeeded, and then removes the source file from that Drive folder.

This tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in to it.
